# IQ revision proposal (for approval)

Draft **0–100 IQ** for draft clashes. Each score is a **blend of all four pillars** (not “best pillar wins”). **100** ≈ **super high or high average on every type** — manipulation, long schemes, fight adaptation, *and* deep knowledge. One-dimensional geniuses score lower than versatile masterminds even if a single pillar is world-class. **~12–20** ≈ comic-relief or impaired.

## Pillars (how to read a roster IQ)

| Pillar | What counts | Examples on roster |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Knowledge** | Domain mastery, memory, theory, rebuilding systems | Senku, Edward, Julius (arcane), Rimuru (nation-building) |
| **Social** | Manipulation, cold reading, performance, control without raw power | Makima, Aizen, Light (public mask), Lelouch, DIO (charisma) |
| **Fighting IQ** | Techniques + mid-fight adaptation; **ability / weapon systems** (rules, conditions, timing, loadouts) | **Light** (Death Note), **Lelouch** (Geass), Kurapika (Chains), DIO/Jotaro (Stands), Garou, Rimuru (skills), Deku (quirk reads) |
| **Scheming / detective** | Clues, hypotheticals, long games, probability | **L & Light**, Lelouch, Kurapika, Levi, Near-tier absent → L tops investigatively |

**Aizen** is the roster **composite** ceiling (**100**). **Senku** is the clearest **Knowledge specialist** (blended **89**, not 97). **Light** and **Lelouch** are the reference **ability-system masterminds**: scheme + social + using their power as **battle IQ** (Death Note / Geass), sit **95–96** under Aizen. **L vs Light** stay close; L is still peak **investigation-only** pillar. When reviewing the list, anyone who **fights through a rule-based power** got a **Fighting IQ** bump where it was missing.
